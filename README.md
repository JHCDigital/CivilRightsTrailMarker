# Introducing the Speaker
## Pat McCollough
Pat McCollough currently serves the City of Jacksonville in the administration of Mayor Donna Deegan as Special Advisor to the Mayor for Civic Engagement and Youth Empowerment. She has played a leading role in the development of the Jacksonville Civil Rights Trail and Legacy Project, including Jacksonville’s successful application for inclusion in the U.S. Civil Rights Trail. Pat is a motivational speaker, author, entrepreneur, educator, strategist, data analyst, community organizer, and civic leader. She holds a bachelor’s degree in education and graphic design technology and recently earned a dual master’s degree in organizational leadership and Artificial Intelligence leadership. Pat has worked at the local, state, and national levels on a wide range of issues and initiatives, from healthcare reform to Wall Street reform. Pat has a passion for empowering young people and ensuring that they have a seat at the table, a voice at the table, and are decision-makers at the table. Above all, Pat is considered a change maker.
